## Project 2: Los Angeles Crime Dashboard

**Description:**  
An **interactive Power BI dashboard** built to analyse crime trends in Los Angeles. The dashboard provides insights into crime categories, hotspots, and time-based patterns, helping stakeholders better understand public safety dynamics and resource allocation needs.

**Data Source & Cleaning:**  
- The dataset included crime categories, locations, dates, and times of occurrence.  
- Cleaning Steps:  
  1. Removed duplicate and inconsistent entries.  
  2. Standardised categorical fields (crime types, area names).  
  3. Formatted date/time columns for temporal analysis.  
  4. Filtered out incomplete or corrupted records.  

**Key Visualizations & Insights:**  
- **Total Crimes:** KPI card tracking total reported incidents.  
- **Crime by Category:** Bar/column chart highlighting the most frequent crime types.  
- **Time-based Trends:** Line/area chart showing daily/monthly fluctuations in crime.  
- **Geographical Hotspots:** Map visualisation pinpointing high-crime neighbourhoods.  
- **Crime by Day/Hour:** Heatmap to identify peak crime hours and weekdays.  
- **Filter Options:** Interactive slicers for crime type, area, and time period.  

**Design Considerations:**  
- Focused on **usability** with clear filters for drill-down analysis.  
- Used **color-coded visuals** to differentiate crime categories.  
- Balanced **overview KPIs** with detailed trend analysis for decision support.  

**Impact:**  
- Provided law enforcement & policymakers with data-backed insights on crime distribution.  
- Highlighted hotspots and peak times, enabling smarter allocation of patrol resources.  
- Future scope includes integrating socio-economic data for deeper cause-and-effect analysis.  

**Tools Used:**  
- Microsoft Power BI – dashboard design & visualisation  
- Power Query / Excel – data cleaning & preparation
